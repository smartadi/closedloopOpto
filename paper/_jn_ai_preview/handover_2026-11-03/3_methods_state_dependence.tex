\ai{\emph{State dependence} (Fig.~\ref{fig:figure4}D).}
\ai{Within each session we standardized each state over that session's trials (open and closed loop together), $z = (x - \bar{x})/\sigma_x$, so that slopes are per within-session standard deviation (SD) of the state and can be compared across states and sessions.}
\ai{In each session we fitted ordinary least-squares lines of steady-state RMSE on $z$, separately for open- and closed-loop trials, and tested each slope across sessions as above; the open-loop (OL) and closed-loop (CL) slopes are in $\%\,\Delta F/F$ of RMSE per SD.}
\ai{For the CL/OL ratio we fitted $\log \mathrm{RMSE}$ on $z$ in each condition and took the difference of the two slopes, $A = b_{\mathrm{CL}} - b_{\mathrm{OL}}$, which we tested across sessions in the same way.}
\ai{$A$ is the slope of the log ratio of fitted (geometric-mean) closed- to open-loop RMSE, the fraction of open-loop error left by the loop and the empirical counterpart of the sensitivity function.}
\ai{We report $100\,(e^{\bar{A}} - 1)$, the percentage change in the CL/OL ratio per SD, with its 95\% confidence interval transformed the same way.}
\ai{We call a state more controllable when it increased open-loop error but not closed-loop error, so that the CL/OL ratio decreased; this is an empirical property of the loop, not controllability in the state-space sense of control theory.}
\ai{As a control for the power measures, which span the stimulation period, we repeated the fits with power measured over the 5\,s before onset, with the laser off, the same length as the main window; these fits are outside the Holm families below.}
\ai{The open-minus-closed-loop gap in mean RMSE, and the open- minus the closed-loop slope (gap trend), are listed in Table~\ref{tab:fig4_state_stats} but not interpreted: the gap is the result of Fig.~\ref{fig:figure3}, and the gap can widen through an increasing open-loop error as much as through a constant closed-loop error.}
\ai{The error decomposition of Fig.~\ref{fig:figure4}C is descriptive and carries no test.}
